# Worksheet: Appointment Finder

Print this page, find a pencil, and take ten unhurried minutes. This
worksheet finds your likely path — it never chooses a benefit for you.

*Education and paperwork help only — never medical, legal, financial, or
benefits advice.*

## Step 2 — Match your circled item to the likely path

(From [Which Appointment You Likely Need](../02-which-appointment.md).)

| If you circled... | Likely path |
| --- | --- |
| Retirement / spouse / disability / Medicare-only | Online first (ssa.gov/apply); phone or office if you'd rather talk |
| Survivor | Phone (1-800-772-1213) or field office — no online path |
| Card / letter / account | Your my Social Security account — no appointment needed |
| A question about a letter | Bring the letter: phone first, then office with the letter in hand |
| Something unusual | Ask for an appointment, phone or in person — say "I'd like to talk it over with someone" |
